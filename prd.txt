NAMI International School offers a diverse and balanced curriculum for Grade (I-VII),
remaining within the framework of the Nepal Government's National Curriculum.
At NAMI International School, we believe in experiential learning, fostering a collaborative
environment where teachers emphasize teamwork to facilitate learning. Beyond English,
students also acquire proficiency in Science and Social Studies taught through
Multidisciplinary and Transdisciplinary themes. Mandarin is introduced as a Foreign
Language from Grade I to Grade VII. Mathematics centres around problem-solving.

In addition to starting computer instruction in the first grade, each classroom is equipped
with an interactive board featuring the latest technology and educational materials. We
prioritize preparing our students for effective communication in the digital age by not only
providing access to the latest ICT tools but also instructing them on how to navigate digital
technology and resources. Our art lessons focus on nurturing creativity. In fine arts classes,
students engage in activities such as painting, sketching, and clay modelling. They also build
confidence in performing arts through participation in acting, singing, dancing, and playing
musical instruments. Our physical education program is designed to help students not only
develop athletic skills but also gain an understanding of the values and principles
associated with physical education. This way, they can commit to a lifelong dedication to
fitness.

SERVICE LEARNING- Our goal at NAMI International School is to help each student find their
own unique way to make a positive impact on the world by instilling in them a love of service
that will last a lifetime. Coordination, leadership, and effective communication are some of
the abilities that students learn, along with techniques for achieving their service goals.
Students develop, launch, and finish their initiatives under the supervision of adults.
Students are required to document their project progress through reflective essays, diaries,
and journals. The end goal is for the youngsters to grow up to be loving, compassionate
people who can see the good in every situation, find creative ways to solve problems and
answer the call to action when they see one.

International & National Collaborations

NAMI has been actively pursuing strategic collaborations with internationally renowned
academic institutions and organisations. Some of these initiatives include partnerships with the
Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) aimed at advancing joint research, innovation, knowledge
exchange, and specialised training programmes. The institute is likewise working toward
establishing formal academic cooperation with Kirk University, Thailand in order to promote
regional academic engagement and institutional collaboration.

The Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Naaya Aayam Multi-Disciplinary Institute
(NAMI), Nepal and Dusit Thani College (DTC), Thailand establishes a strategic academic
collaboration to promote cooperation in hospitality education, training, and capacity
development. The collaboration focuses on delivering industry-relevant education,
strengthening academic standards, and exploring future opportunities for knowledge exchange
and international engagement between the two institutions.

The Council for Technical Education and Vocational Training (CTEVT), Nepal has granted approval
to NAMI on 2026 to conduct five short-term vocational training programmes: General Cook
Commis II (484 Hrs), Hotel Assistant (390 Hrs.), Room Attendant (390 Hrs.), Barista (390 Hrs.),
and Bar Tender (390 Hrs.).
Furthermore, NAMI is engaged in ongoing consultations with national and international
universities to develop comprehensive frameworks for students and faculty exchange
programmes, collaborative research initiatives, and entrepreneurship development. In parallel,
the institute continues to collaborate with a wide range of governmental and non-governmental
organisations at both national and international levels to undertake initiatives and programmes
that are aligned with its academic mission, institutional priorities, and broader commitment to
social and educational advancement.

Academic Infrastructure and Digital Growth

In response to the evolving demands of contemporary education, Naaya Aayam Multi-
Disciplinary Institute (NAMI) is investing in the establishment of a comprehensive Digital Library
and e-Learning Hub. These initiatives are designed to provide students and faculty members with
enhanced access to a broad range of academic resources, digital databases, and learning
materials, while also supporting flexible and technology-driven blended learning approaches
across all academic programmes.

In parallel, the institute is undertaking significant infrastructure development to strengthen its
physical and academic environment. This includes the expansion and modernisation of learning
spaces, well-equipped classrooms, advanced science and computer laboratories, and specialised
training facilities designed to support practical, research-oriented education. Emphasis is also
being placed on the integration of smart classroom technologies, improved connectivity
infrastructure, and student-centric learning environments that foster collaboration and
innovation.

Study Abroad and Exchange Program

NAMI is introducing new initiatives to promote international mobility for students and faculty.
Strategic academic partnerships will allow students to participate in study abroad programmes,
international residencies, and cross-cultural academic exchanges. These experiences are
intended to broaden perspectives and enhance academic excellence. As part of the faculty
exchange and professional development programme, several NAMI faculty members
participated in academic exchange visits aimed at enhancing teaching practices, subject
knowledge, and international collaboration. In February 2024, Rajendra Acharya (English General
Paper), Reshma Rana (Psychology), and Shreehari Timilsina (Biology) took part in the programme
from 12-13 February 2024 at GEMS School, Dhapakhel, Lalitpur. Similarly, in March 2025,
Jayprakash Mishra (Physics), Niroj Duwal (Economics), and Sahadev Jaishi (Computer Science)
participated in the exchange programme from 6-7 March 2025 at Global College of
Management, Mid Baneshwor, Kathmandu. These engagements provided valuable opportunities
for faculty members to share best practices, gain exposure to innovative pedagogical approaches,
and strengthen academic partnerships, contributing to the continuous enhancement of teaching
and learning at NAMI.

Research & Development and Programme Expansion
NAMI is expanding its academic offerings to include high- demand disciplines such as Artificial
Intelligence, Data Science, Green Entrepreneurship, and Digital Business. NAMI is also enhancing
its online learning platforms to deliver accessible, high-quality education to students across Nepal
and beyond. NAMI will be introducing the Bachelor's Program in Environmental Studies, from
August 2026 affiliated with Kathmandu University.

NAMI is planning to establish a Research and Development Wing, which is essential for fostering
innovation, improving academic quality, and guiding evidence-based decision-making within the
institution. It will help generate new knowledge, address real-world challenges, and strengthen
teaching and learning through research-driven practices. The R&D Wing will also enhance
institutional reputation by promoting publications, collaborations, and impactful projects, while
attracting funding and partnerships from national and international organizations. In addition, it
will nurture a strong research culture among students and faculty, encouraging critical thinking,
creativity, and continuous improvement, ultimately contributing to the long-term growth and
relevance of the institution. Coordination with all three tiers of government, INGO's/NGOs,
private sector and other stakeholders will be key to its success.

Sustainability and Green Initiatives

Sustainability is a core principle at NAMI, embedded in its operations, academic framework, and
strategic development. The institute promotes the use of renewable energy, efficient resource
management, structured waste segregation and recycling systems, reduced reliance on paper-
based processes, sustainable mobility across the campus, and the establishment of a plastic-free
zone.

These initiatives are aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, particularly
those related to climate action, responsible consumption and production, and quality education.
Sustainability is further embedded in academic delivery through interdisciplinary curricula,
applied research, and community engagement initiatives addressing environmental challenges.
Through this integrated approach, NAMI aims to reduce its environmental footprint while
nurturing graduates who are environmentally responsible and equipped to contribute
meaningfully to sustainable development at local, national and global levels.

Emergency Response and Disaster Management Plan

NAMI is undertaking an initiative to develop a comprehensive Emergency Response and Disaster
Management Plan for its educational entities, considering the high vulnerability of Kathmandu
Valley to earthquakes and other natural hazards. The initiative aims to strengthen institutional
preparedness and enhance the safety and well-being of students, faculty members, staff, and
visitors during emergencies.

As part of this initiative, NAMI plans to collaborate with organizations working in the fields of
disaster risk reduction, emergency preparedness, and humanitarian response to conduct
awareness and capacity-building programs. The institution also intends to organize emergency
preparedness exercises, including earthquake preparedness and evacuation drills, for students,
teachers, and administrative personnel.
These activities are expected to significantly contribute to fostering a culture of safety,
preparedness, and resilience across all NAMI entities while strengthening their capacity to
effectively prepare for, respond to, and recover from emergencies and disasters.

Incubation Centre

NAMI has established Naaya Aayam Avinaya Prerana Kendra, a dedicated Innovation and
Incubation Centre designed to transform ideas into impactful ventures. Established in
collaboration with Startup Discovery Asia (SDA), a leading startup ecosystem enabler in South
Asia, the centre provides aspiring entrepreneurs with access to mentorship, networking
opportunities, business development support, and industry expertise. SDA is known for
empowering young innovators and startups through incubation, acceleration, capacity-building
programs, and connections with investors, industry leaders, and development partners.

The incubation centre serves as a dynamic platform where students can develop innovative
solutions to real-world challenges. Whether pursuing a technology startup, social enterprise,
research-based innovation, creative venture, or sustainable business idea, students receive
guidance and resources to refine, test, and scale their concepts.

Key features of the centre include:

    � Entrepreneurial training and capacity-building workshops
    � Mentorship from experienced entrepreneurs, academics, and industry experts
    � Startup ideation, business model development, and validation support
    � Access to networking events, innovation challenges, and startup competitions
    � Opportunities for collaboration with industry, investors, and development organizations
    � Support for prototype development, market research, and venture growth
    � A collaborative workspace that encourages creativity, teamwork, and innovation
Through Naaya Aayam Avinaya Prerana Kendra, NAMI is cultivating a culture of innovation and
entrepreneurship, empowering students to become future business leaders, change-makers, and
job creators. By combining academic excellence with practical entrepreneurial experience, the
institute equips students with the skills, confidence, and opportunities needed to thrive in an
increasingly dynamic and competitive global economy.

Alumni Network

NAMI has built a robust and growing alumni community. To date, over 5,000 students have
graduated from the NEB 10+2 programme, more than 500 from the A-Level programme, and over
2,500 from undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. Many of these alumni have gone on
to pursue successful careers in business, technology, science, and education both within Nepal
and internationally. NAMI continues to engage with its alumni through networking events,
mentorship programmes, and professional development opportunities. NAMI is also collecting
their success stories which can motivate our current and prospective students and provide
valuable insights into the opportunities that can emerge through dedication and hard work.

Leadership Development and Career Counselling

NAMI has been giving priority to career counselling and leadership development as a key area of
student support, aiming to assist students in making informed career choices while building
essential leadership competencies. The initiative provides structured career guidance, skills
assessment, mentorship, and exposure to diverse academic and professional pathways,
alongside training, workshops, and experiential learning to enhance confidence, communication,
and decision-making skills. In addition, NAMI has launched a monthly motivational series titled
"Motivational Stories by Inspirational Leaders of Change," featuring successful dignitaries from
different fields, which inspires students from secondary to Master's level through real-life
experiences and insights beyond classroom learning. Similarly, The NAMI Golden Z Club
Orientation Program supports leadership development by exposing students to global leadership
values and women's empowerment initiatives through interaction with experienced Zontians. It
helps build key skills such as communication, teamwork, confidence, and decision-making, while
encouraging active participation in community-based activities. The program also fosters ethical
leadership, responsibility, and a sense of social contribution among students.
